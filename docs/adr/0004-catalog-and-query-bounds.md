# 0004. Catalog and query bounds

Catalog downloads stop above 16 MiB. Downloads write chunks into one preallocated buffer and return a view over the received bytes. Indexed strings fit in 1 KiB of UTF-8; other retained strings fit in 16 KiB, collections have at most 128 entries, and normalized rows fit in 64 KiB of serialized JSON. Upserts contain at most 100 rows or 512 KiB of serialized JSON. These limits leave headroom for generated fields, price history and mutation serialization.

Catalog mutations clear ETag and body hash while renewing the lease. Completed data syncs restore source, ETag and hash together; failures after writes cannot reuse validators from an earlier catalog. Removal uses the stored keys read before upserts. Before catalog writes, an unforced run that would make more than 20% of stored priced models unpriced finishes as partial without changing catalog rows. Malformed providers and models preserve their stored rows. Usable-provider and usable-model floors still apply; these floors also bound repeated removal across runs. Failed source attempts remain ordered fixed-message diagnostics, including when fallback succeeds.

List pages require 1–100 items, read at most `numItems + 1` rows and target at most 1 MiB of document bytes. A document can reach or cross the byte target. Pinned intervals interrupted by those bounds return split metadata. Equivalent-model queries sort priced rows before unpriced rows, then by the required `costOrder` field in descending order. `costOrder` is negated blended price for priced rows and zero for unpriced rows.
